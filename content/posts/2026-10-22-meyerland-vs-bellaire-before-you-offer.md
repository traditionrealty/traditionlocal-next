Before making an offer in Meyerland or Bellaire, confirm three things for the exact address: the rules that govern changes to the home, the official flood map and the seller's flood history, and the property tax step you will take after closing. Meyerland homes follow section specific deed restrictions, while Bellaire handles its own permits and floodplain rules. Knowing which rule book applies helps you price any renovation plans before you commit.

## The quick comparison

### Meyerland

Find your section first, then read the deed restrictions that apply to that address.

### Bellaire

Call the city's Development Services before you budget an addition or major remodel.

## Meyerland: start with your section

The Meyerland Community Improvement Association posts [22 sets of deed restrictions](https://meyerland.net/deed-restrictions/) for different parts of the neighborhood. Its [find my section tool](https://meyerland.net/about/find-my-section-tool/) matches a house number and street to the section and restriction document for that home. Read that document before you offer, not after.

If you plan to renovate, also review the association's [construction policies](https://meyerland.net/mcia-rcc-policies/). They say trees between the front property line and the front setback need committee approval before removal, and that required City of Houston permits must be given to the committee.

**A useful stop:** Meyerland Park, 5151 Jason, appears on [Houston's list of city parks](https://www.houstontx.gov/parks/parksitesG-N.html). Try the trip from each home on your shortlist.

## Bellaire: check the city's floodplain rules

Bellaire runs its own approvals. The city's [floodplain development page](https://www.bellairetx.gov/1004/Development-in-the-Floodplain) says all development in Bellaire must obtain the necessary permits, and it asks owners to contact Development Services at 713.662.8230 before building or filling.

Pay attention to the 50 percent rule on that page. When improvements cost 50 percent or more of the structure's market value, the home must meet new building requirements, including construction above the base flood elevation. If a big remodel is part of your plan, ask about it before you set your offer price.

**A useful stop:** Bellaire Town Square Family Aquatic Center, 7001 Fifth Street. Check the [city's posted hours](https://www.bellairetx.gov/720/Aquatics), since the schedule changes between seasons.

## Before you write the offer

- Pull the deed restrictions or city rules for the exact address.
- Look up the address on FEMA's effective flood map, using [HCFCD's flood mapping guide](https://www.hcfcd.org/Resources/Interactive-Mapping-Tools/Harris-County-Flood-Education).
- Read every flood answer on the Seller's Disclosure Notice.
- Ask whether past additions had the required approvals and permits.
- Plan to file your homestead exemption after you move in.

## A few common questions

### What flood questions does a Texas seller have to answer?

Since September 1, 2019, the seller's notice asks whether the home is in a 100 year or 500 year floodplain, whether it has flooded, whether the seller filed a flood insurance claim, and whether they received FEMA or SBA flood assistance, according to the [Senate bill analysis](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/86R/analysis/html/SB00339F.htm). TREC says the [Seller's Disclosure Notice](https://www.trec.texas.gov/forms/sellers-disclosure-notice) is required for previously occupied single family homes.

### Is the Harris County flood map the official one?

No. HCFCD says its [Flood Education Mapping Tool](https://www.hcfcd.org/Resources/Interactive-Mapping-Tools/Harris-County-Flood-Education) is not the effective FIRM, and it points you to FEMA's map service for the official version. HCFCD also posts a [conditional map](https://www.hcfcd.org/Activity/Projects/Brays-Bayou/C-11-Project-Brays/Floodplain-Maps) showing potential floodplain improvements once Project Brays is complete.

### Does the City of Houston enforce deed restrictions?

It can help. The city's [legal department](https://www.houstontx.gov/legal/dr-faq.html) says it is authorized to help enforce recorded deed restrictions, which are filed with the county clerk and can differ by subdivision, section, or even lot.

### How do I file a homestead exemption in Harris County?

HCAD lets you [file through its mobile app](https://hcad.org/hcad-online-services/homestead/) using your move in date and a Texas driver's license.
