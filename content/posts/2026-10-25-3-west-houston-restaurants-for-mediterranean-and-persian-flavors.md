For Mediterranean and Persian dining in West Houston, put Empire Turkish Grill, Cafe Benedicte, and Cafe Caspian on your list. These restaurants span Memorial Drive and Westheimer, so choose the one that fits your evening rather than treating them as stops on a walking route. All three have reported Google ratings above four stars.

## Empire Turkish Grill: Start With Something to Share

12448 Memorial Dr., Houston, TX 77024 · 4.5★ on Google

Empire Turkish Grill is a Memorial Drive option for exploring Turkish food through appetizers and grilled meats. Google reviewers mention the mixed appetizer selection, including hummus, ezme, and lebni, as a way to begin before moving on to kebabs.

If you are deciding between several meats, ask about the mixed grill. It is a useful starting point for a table that wants to explore the menu together. Save a little room for dessert, too: reviewers mention baklava and Turkish puddings. Check the current menu before settling on a particular combination.

[Website](https://www.empireturkishgrill.com/) · [Menu](https://www.empireturkishgrill.com/takeout-menu/) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=12448%20Memorial%20Dr.%2C%20Houston%2C%20TX%2077024%20Empire%20Turkish%20Grill)

## Cafe Benedicte: A Mediterranean Menu With Room for Variety

15455 Memorial Dr., Suite 100, Houston, TX 77079 · 4.7★ on Google

In the Energy Corridor, Cafe Benedicte offers a broad menu that can help when everyone wants something different. Start with baked brie wrapped in puff pastry with walnuts and fig jam, or keep the opening order simple with hummus and naan.

For dinner, consider the chicken tagine, which brings together chicken, apricot, almonds, and raisins with basmati rice and green beans. Seafood fans can look at the Spanish style paella. The menu also includes salads, sandwiches, and pasta, making this a practical choice for a group with several different cravings.

[Website](https://cafebenedicte.com/) · [Menu](https://cafebenedicte.com/menu) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=15455%20Memorial%20Dr.%2C%20Suite%20100%2C%20Houston%2C%20TX%2077079%20Cafe%20Benedicte)

## Cafe Caspian: Persian Kebabs, Rice, and a Reason to Return

12126 Westheimer Rd., Suite 98A, Houston, TX 77077 · 4.5★ on Google

Cafe Caspian on Westheimer focuses on Persian cooking. For a first visit, consider koobideh, seasoned ground beef or chicken shaped around skewers and charbroiled. If you want to compare preparations, the menu includes combinations of chicken chenjeh and beef koobideh.

Take a look beyond the kebabs as well. Ghormeh sabzi combines herbs, kidney beans, dried limes, and beef, while fesenjan pairs chicken with pomegranate and walnut sauce. For dessert, saffron ice cream with pistachios and rosewater offers another introduction to the flavors on the menu.

[Website](https://cafecaspian.com/) · [Menu](https://cafecaspian.com/cafecaspianmenu/) · [Directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=12126%20Westheimer%20Rd.%2C%20Suite%2098A%2C%20Houston%2C%20TX%2077077%20Cafe%20Caspian)

Choose Empire for Turkish appetizers and grills, Cafe Benedicte for a broad Mediterranean dinner menu, or Cafe Caspian to explore Persian kebabs and stews. Pick one for this outing and keep the others for another evening.
